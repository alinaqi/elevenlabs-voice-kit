# Overview: Voice Kit

**Goal:** any Voice of the Oasis team (12 teams, 24 h, 3 challenges, EN + AR) gets a working ElevenLabs voice agent in minutes, from one key.

**Challenges (from the Judge & Mentor Guide v2):**
1. AI Voice Navigator (robot A): followable directions, clarifying questions, mid-conversation language switch, short answers. Stretch: summit agenda, route to phone, memory within the session.
2. Soft Landing Guide (software): questions before advice, setup route, per-founder checklist, booked human call. Stretch: written summary, side-by-side options, more languages.
3. HALA Concierge (robot B): at least 5 services end to end, booking details, spoken confirmation, handover. Stretch: reschedule/cancel, follow-up, impatient callers, phone line.

**Judging:** working demo 25%, ElevenLabs depth 20%, voice UX 20%, challenge fit 15%, pitch 10%, investment 10%.
